Answer one question at a time: product, industry, departments, level, harness (only for products that have harnesses), then the topics. Each answer changes the topics we recommend next. Times are estimates to help you plan, never limits.

How recommendations, levels, and prerequisite recaps work

Topics are pre-selected only when they follow directly from the vertical or departments you chose, and each card shows why. With no vertical or department, nothing is pre-selected — check individual topics or choose Select recommended to add our suggested baseline.
